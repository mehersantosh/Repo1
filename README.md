# Digital Health Transformation Playbook

This repository captures practical frameworks, checklists, and playbooks
for driving Digital Health transformation in hospitals and healthcare networks.

It is based on real-world experience in:
- Hospital IT leadership & commissioning
- EMR / EHR adoption
- Clinical system stabilization
- Interoperability, governance, and compliance
- Scaling digital health initiatives across institutions

## Who this is for
- Hospital CIOs / IT Heads
- Digital Health Leaders
- Public health & national health programs
- Healthcare administrators driving transformation

## Contents
- Digital maturity assessment framework
- Hospital IT baseline readiness checklist
- EMR adoption & clinician enablement strategy
- Digital governance & interoperability approach
- 30-60-90 day digital transformation roadmap
